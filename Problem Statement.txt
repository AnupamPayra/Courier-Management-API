Problem statement:

A local courier company is struggling to manage parcel bookings, customer information, delivery assignments, and parcel tracking.

Currently, they maintain parcel records manually. Customers frequently contact the company to ask about delivery status, while employees struggle to track which parcels are pending, dispatched, or delivered.

Your task is to build a Courier Management API using FastAPI that digitizes this entire process.

The API should allow customers to book parcels, track shipments, and view their booking history. Employees should be able to manage shipments, update delivery statuses, and monitor courier operations.
2. Core requirements
Your application should have these functionalities:

A. Customer management
- Register new customers.
- Retrieve customer information.
- Update customer details.
- Delete customer accounts (consider existing shipments before deletion).

B. Parcel management
- Customers can create parcel bookings.
- Each parcel should have a unique tracking ID.
- Store sender and receiver information.
- Store parcel weight, dimensions, pickup address, and destination.
- Retrieve parcel details.

C. Shipment tracking
- Customers can track parcels using their tracking ID.
- Employees can update shipment status.
- Maintain a history of every status change.

Possible shipment statuses:

BOOKED → PICKED_UP → IN_TRANSIT → OUT_FOR_DELIVERY → DELIVERED

Also handle:
- CANCELLED
- FAILED_DELIVERY
- RETURNED

D. Employee management
- Register employees.
- Assign parcels to delivery employees.
- Retrieve all parcels assigned to a particular employee.
- Update delivery information.

E. Dashboard / Analytics
- Total parcels booked.
- Total delivered parcels.
- Pending shipments.
- Failed deliveries.
- Average delivery time.
